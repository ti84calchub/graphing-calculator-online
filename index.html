How to use a TI-84 calculator, from the very first button

Whether your teacher handed you a TI-84 Plus or you bought the newer TI-84 Plus CE, the layout is almost the same. This guide walks through it in the order beginners actually need it, from switching on to solving your first equation on a graph.

1. Know your model

The TI-84 Plus has a black-and-white screen and runs on four AAA batteries. The TI-84 Plus CE is thinner, has a backlit colour screen and a rechargeable battery that charges over USB. The keys and menus are nearly identical, so this guide works for both. If a screen looks slightly different on your calculator, it is usually just the operating system version.

2. A tour of the keypad

  • Top row (Y=, WINDOW, ZOOM, TRACE, GRAPH): everything for graphing.
  • Editing keys (2nd, MODE, DEL, the arrow pad): move around, delete and change settings.
  • Menu keys (MATH, APPS, PRGM, VARS, STAT): open lists of extra functions.
  • Function keys (x⁻¹, SIN, COS, TAN, ^, x², LOG, LN): scientific functions.
  • Number pad with the four operations, (−) and ENTER at the bottom right.

3. The 2nd and ALPHA keys

Almost every key has two extra jobs printed above it. Press 2nd first to use the blue label, or ALPHA to type the green letter. You don't hold them down: press, release, then press the next key. The most useful 2nd functions for beginners are √ (above x²), π (above ^), QUIT (above MODE), ANS (above (−)) and ENTRY (above ENTER).

4. Your first calculations

The TI-84 follows the normal order of operations, so 2+3×4 gives 14. Use parentheses whenever you are unsure. Two habits save a lot of marks: use (−) for negative numbers and the − key only for subtraction, and remember that −3² is −9 while (−3)² is 9.

5. Fractions and decimals

To turn a decimal answer into a fraction, press MATH and choose 1:▶Frac. On the TI-84 Plus CE you can also type stacked fractions: press ALPHA Y= to open the FRAC shortcut menu and choose the n/d template.

6. The MODE settings that matter

Most beginners only ever change one setting: RADIAN or DEGREE. Geometry and most trigonometry homework uses degrees; calculus uses radians. If a trig answer looks strange, check MODE first. Leave the other settings on their defaults unless your teacher says otherwise.

7. Graphing your first function

Press Y=, type the right-hand side of your equation using X,T,θ,n for x, then press ZOOM 6:ZStandard. To read exact points, press TRACE or open the table with 2nd GRAPH. To find where a graph crosses the x-axis, use 2nd TRACE → 2:zero; to find where two graphs meet, use 5:intersect.

8. Errors you will meet (and how to fix them)

ErrorWhat it usually means
ERR:SYNTAXSubtraction key used for a negative number, or a missing bracket. Choose Goto to jump to the problem.
ERR:DOMAINThe input is not allowed, such as √ of a negative number or log of zero.
ERR:DIVIDE BY 0Something divides by zero.
ERR:WINDOW RANGEXmin is not less than Xmax (or Ymin not less than Ymax).
ERR:INVALID DIMA stat plot is on but its list is empty. Turn the plot off in Y= or 2nd Y=.

9. Looking after your calculator

Charge the TI-84 Plus CE before a test, or carry spare AAA batteries for the TI-84 Plus. If the calculator freezes or settings get messy, you can reset the RAM with 2nd + (MEM) → 7:Reset → 1:All RAM. This deletes lists and programs in RAM, so save anything important first.

10. Older models: TI-83 and TI-85

If you were given an older calculator, the TI-83 calculator uses almost the same keys and menus as the TI-84, so every lesson above carries over with only small screen differences. The TI-85 calculator is older and organised differently, with soft-key menus along the bottom of the screen. It still graphs and calculates well, but expect a short learning curve if you switch between it and a TI-84.

Practise, then try it for real

The fastest way to learn how to use a TI-84 calculator is repetition: run each lesson in the key trainer, take the quiz until you score 8/8, then repeat the same keystrokes on your own handheld or on an online graphing calculator. After a week of short practice sessions the keys become second nature.

TI 84 calculator beginner FAQ

How do I use a TI-84 calculator for the first time?

Press ON, then 2nd and MODE (QUIT) to reach the home screen. Type a calculation such as 12*(3+4) and press ENTER. From there, learn the 2nd key, the (−) key and the Y= and GRAPH keys.

What does the 2nd key do on a TI-84?

The 2nd key uses the blue function printed above each key. For example 2nd then x² gives the square root, 2nd then ^ gives π, and 2nd then MODE takes you back to the home screen.

Why does my TI-84 say ERR:SYNTAX?

Usually a minus sign was typed with the subtraction key where a negative number was needed, or a parenthesis is missing. Choose Goto to jump to the mistake, fix it and press ENTER again.

How do I put my TI-84 in degree mode?

Press MODE, move down to the RADIAN DEGREE line, highlight DEGREE and press ENTER. Then press 2nd and MODE to return to the home screen.

How do I turn a decimal into a fraction on a TI-84?

Type the decimal, press MATH, choose 1:▶Frac and press ENTER. For example .75▶Frac gives 3/4.

How do I graph an equation on a TI-84?

Press Y=, type the equation using the X,T,θ,n key for X, then press ZOOM and choose 6:ZStandard to see it in a standard window. Press TRACE to read points on the graph.

How do I reset a TI-84 calculator?

Press 2nd and + to open MEM, choose 7:Reset, then 1:All RAM and confirm. This clears programs, lists and settings in RAM, so only do it when you really need a fresh start.

Can I practise TI-84 keystrokes online?

Yes. The key trainer on this page highlights every key in order for common tasks, and the quiz tests whether you can find each function. For a full graphing calculator online, use our main TI 84 calculator.
